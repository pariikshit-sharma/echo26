About the Event

What is ECHO 2026?

ECHO 2026 is a grand celebration of art and creativity, organised by the School of Art & Architecture, BFA Program at Sushant University on 23rd October — marking International Artist Day.

The event features a book launch, a national-level art exhibition, panel discussions, artist-led talks, and brand stalls. A special highlight is the keynote address and book launch by the President and CEO of the Think and Grow Rich Institute, Canada.
